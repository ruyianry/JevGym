Measuring the Jev-series models' capacity

A timestamped benchmark and trading arena for real-world probabilistic forecasting. Every prediction uses only the information that existed at the time, then is scored against what actually happened — calibration and realized profit.

Why prediction markets? They quote a direct probability for every event — continuously updated, and directly comparable to a Jev model's output (probability in, probability out).
