Can you beat Jev?

Six Kalshi questions in mixed order — storms, temperature, the Fed, crypto — all ones you can't just recall (or still unresolved). Set your probability with the slider, lock it in, and see who was closest: you, Jev, or the market. Changed your mind? Hit revert.

JevGym in miniature. A market price is just the crowd's probability. Whoever lands closest to the truth (lowest Brier score) is the one who could readjust the market toward reality — that's the point (not extracting arbitrage, but correcting the price). Move each slider to your P(YES), lock it in, and we reveal Jev's real answer, the market price, and what actually happened. Some questions have no answer yet (pending or genuinely ambiguous) — those show where you and Jev stand but aren't scored, because forecasting means committing before you can look it up. Hit Try again to re-take any question. Return = P&L as a % of the $1 contract if you'd bet your side at the market price.

Want to ask Jev your own questions on Kalshi?

This page uses Jev's real, pre-computed answers — nothing leaves your browser and no API key is needed here. To run Jev live on questions you pick, use the open-source CLI locally and keep your key in a .env file — the safest place for it. Never paste an API key into a web page: it can't safely reach the paid API from a browser anyway (CORS), and a page should never see your secret.

echo "TS_TOKEN=your-key" >> .env      # gitignored; stays on your machine
jevgym eval --agents kalshi_market,jev --track both

Weather is a real leakage-safe JevGym result. The others use Jev's real live answers; where a past-event market price is shown it's an approximate crowd estimate from the time (illustrative). The no-answer questions are genuinely open or ambiguous.
